# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews or
anywhere else it is discussed, is expected to:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Linux, Asahi
  or open source.
- Assume good intent and give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect different opinions, experience levels and backgrounds.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Sharing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Any other behaviour a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer,
[@antialberteinstein](https://github.com/antialberteinstein), through GitHub.
Reports are handled privately and as quickly as possible.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that break this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful to the community.

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, and when someone is
officially representing the project elsewhere.
